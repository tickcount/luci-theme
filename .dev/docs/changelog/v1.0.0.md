## Outline 1.0.0

A ChatGPT-style redesign of the whole theme.

### Design

- ChatGPT settings look across every LuCI page: pill controls, hairline cards, secondary-ink descriptions, one-surface dialogs.
- Geist and Geist Mono type, ChatGPT palette for dark and light mode.
- Status pills, zone dots, Lucide icons in place of LuCI's Tango set.

### Sidebar and search

- Icon rail plus a panel that shows one section at a time.
- ChatGPT-style ⌘K search dialog for every page and tab.

### Charts

- Realtime graphs as Analytics cards: legend, range switcher, dithered fills.
- Themed nlbw doughnuts, Channel Analysis palette, Statistics and vnStat graph cards.

### Login

- Centred column with pill fields, new logo and footer.

### Fixes

- Layout fixes from a full sweep of pages, tabs and dialogs (DHCP, Wireless, Interfaces, Mount Points, Software, SSH keys, Diagnostics and more).

### Upgrading

The theme is now named `outline` (`/luci-static/outline`). Saved preferences (dark mode, collapsed sidebar, search history) carry over automatically.
