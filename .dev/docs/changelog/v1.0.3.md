## Outline 1.0.3

Codex-style cards and menus, ChatGPT's page header, and a round of layout fixes.

### Changes

- Settings cards take Codex's panel fill (`#0a0a0a` on black, white on the light canvas), so the hairline and the row dividers read as one line.
- Menus are Codex's dropdown overlay: 90% elevated fill behind a blur, a hairline ring plus a soft shadow; the focused item shows the hover fill instead of a focus ring.
- Native selects (Firewall › Input / Output / Forward, …) open the same themed menu in Chromium 135+ via the customizable select; other browsers keep the system popup.
- Page title matches ChatGPT's settings header: 24/28.8 medium, 32px from the top, 48px above the first section; a page description sits right under it.
- Status › Overview: port tiles fill the row, left-aligned, with the network dot in the title ("● lan1"); Network / Wireless cards sit on the left edge with their details in a key/value column.
- Login page: the "Please enter your username and password." line is gone.

### Fixes

- Dropdown lists stay inside the content area (and inside dialogs) on every viewport, open in place without a frame at the wrong spot, and close on a click outside in touch-capable desktop browsers.
- Dropdown item descriptions use secondary ink, like page descriptions.
- Section tables: a named table's "Name" title is a muted column title with an unbroken line under the header; a named row's title cell takes the hover fill, and only real edge cells round their corners.
- Backup / Flash Firmware: groups have even spacing, the description sits under its title, and every divider starts at the text edge.
- Overview: the "Status" title no longer renders small and flush against the top.
